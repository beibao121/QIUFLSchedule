Schedule and broadcast data from ESPN, fetched live each time this page loads. Times shown in your device's local time zone; games whose kickoff time hasn't been announced yet show as TBD. "Where to watch" is matched to your apps — Paramount+, Peacock, Netflix, Prime Video, Hulu, Disney+, and Fox One. Games that aren't on any of them say so instead of sending you to an app that wouldn't work. Win chances come from betting odds for NFL games, with the bookmaker's margin taken out, and from ESPN's own projection for Indiana — sportsbooks don't post college lines this far ahead. Either way it's an estimate, not a forecast. Broadcasts can change, so it's worth double-checking close to kickoff.
